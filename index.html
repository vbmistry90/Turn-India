THE CHALLENGE

The Problem

Every year, 50+ million tonnes of electronic waste poison our soil, water, and air.

The world runs on technology built without nature in mind. Outdated electronics leak toxic chemicals, consume excessive energy, and generate mountains of waste that our planet cannot absorb.

We don't need to start over. We need to upgrade what already exists — smarter, cleaner, and in harmony with the natural world.

OUR ANSWER

Our Solution

The technology already exists. What's missing is the will to upgrade it.

TURN India identifies existing products used by billions worldwide and develops nature-aligned upgrades — reducing toxicity, extending lifespan, and cutting energy consumption.

From household electronics to industrial systems, we're building a roadmap for a world where technology and nature thrive together.
