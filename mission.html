WHY WE EXIST

Our Mission

The world's technology was built for convenience — not for the planet.
We're here to change that.

THE STORY

How It Started

Every day, billions of people use electronic products — phones, appliances, industrial machines — that were designed without nature in mind. These products leak toxins, consume excessive energy, and end up as mountains of waste that our planet cannot absorb.

TURN India was born from a simple but powerful idea: we don't need to throw everything away and start over. The world already has the technology. What it needs is the will — and the resources — to upgrade it.

Our team of engineers, environmental scientists, and advocates is developing a systematic roadmap for upgrading the most widely used electronic products on Earth, making them safer, cleaner, and longer-lasting.

OUR APPROACH

How We're Doing It

01

Identify

We map the most widely used electronic products globally and assess their environmental impact — from manufacturing to end of life.

02

Research

Our team develops nature-aligned upgrade pathways: replacing toxic components, improving energy efficiency, and extending product lifespan.

03

Test

We validate practical upgrades and measure real-world improvements before recommending solutions at scale.

04

Scale

With global support, we partner with manufacturers, governments, and communities to implement upgrades at scale — one product category at a time.
